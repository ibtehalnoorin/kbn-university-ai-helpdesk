# Khaja Bandanawaz University  
Educating Humanity  
  
We are a research oriented University focused and dedicated for the betterment of humanity. Looking to contribute through each and every member of the University, making every individual responsible for the good of the nation. After research comes sustainability, confidence building, skill development and physical fitness .  
  
Through carefully designed courses at KBNU, students have innumerable opportunities to emerge successful as Professionals in Medicine, Engineering and Technology, Nursing, Education, Law, Sports, Management and Entrepreneurship, and Religion.

# Why Choose Khaja Bandanawaz University?
Providing unparalleled infrastructure, industry accreditation, and career pathways.
NMC & AICTE Approvals
Accredited and approved by statutory regulatory bodies ensuring valid national and international degree recognition.
Advanced Research Labs
Dedicated research centers in Biotechnology, Molecular Diagnostics, and Advanced Engineering Prototyping.
1500+ Bed Teaching Hospital
Khaja Banda Nawaz Teaching Hospital provides continuous hands-on clinical exposure for medical and nursing students.
Infosys Springboard Partnership
Strategic MoUs with industry leaders for skill development, internships, and guaranteed placement drives.
Pioneering Aeronautical Branch
First university in Kalaburagi region to offer specialized B.E. in Aeronautical Engineering with practical flight modules.
Holistic Campus Life
World-class House of Sports, central digital library, vibrant hostels, and cultural forums.
