Aeronautical Engineering is a branch of engineering that deals with the design, development, testing, and maintenance of aircraft and spacecraft. It involves the study of aerodynamics, propulsion, materials science, control systems, and avionics. The Department of Aeronautical Engineering was established in 2006 and has grown to become one of the important engineering branches at the University.
Courses typically cover aerodynamics, aircraft structures, propulsion systems, control systems, and avionics. Students also learn about materials science, manufacturing processes, and aircraft design.
Specialised areas include Aerodynamics, Aircraft Structures, Propulsion Systems, Control Systems, and Avionics — preparing graduates for roles in aerospace design, aircraft maintenance, propulsion, flight testing, and related fields.

# Career Pathways & Opportunities
Promising professional avenues in aerospace defense, commercial aviation, and R&D agencies
Aerospace Design & Structural Engineer
Roles in aircraft structural modeling, wing geometry optimization, and stress verification with aerospace majors.
Defense R&D & Space Agencies
Prestigious career opportunities in government agencies such as HAL, ISRO, DRDO, NAL, and ADA.
Propulsion Systems Engineer
Design and testing of turbofan engines, gas turbine components, and rocket boosters for defense and space programs.
Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAV) & Drones
Innovative roles in autonomous drone design, flight control programming, and payload system integration.
Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (AME)
Maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO) management for major commercial airlines and air defense fleets.
Flight Test & Aerodynamics Specialist
Testing real/prototype aircraft performance parameters, aerodynamic stability, and air safety compliance.
