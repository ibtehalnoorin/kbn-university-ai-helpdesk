B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce) is an undergraduate degree programme in commerce and business administration. It provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the fundamentals of accounting, finance, economics, and business management.
The three-year programme covers subjects including financial accounting, cost accounting, taxation, business law, economics, marketing, human resource management, and organisational behaviour.
The primary objective is to prepare students for careers in commerce and business management. Graduates are well-equipped to pursue roles in accounting, banking, finance, marketing, and management, as well as professional courses such as CA, CMA, and CS.

##### Eligibility Criteria
A candidate who has passed the two-year Pre-University (PUC) examination conducted by the Pre-University Board of Education, Government of Karnataka, or any other examination considered equivalent by the University is eligible for admission to the first semester of B.Com.
Candidates from other recognized boards (CBSE, ISC, or equivalent 10+2) are eligible, subject to recognition by the University.
Students who have passed relevant Job Oriented Courses (JOC – 10+2) / ITI (10+2) may also be eligible as per University norms.
Note: Admission is generally based on merit in the qualifying examination. Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs, reservation details, and any additional requirements.
