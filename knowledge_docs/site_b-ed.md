# Specialized Areas
Core components of the B.Ed. programme
Hands-on teaching practice in real school settings under guided supervision.
Educational Psychology

##### Eligibility Criteria
Candidates applying for admission to the B.Ed. degree program must fulfill the following qualification requirements:
Candidates must hold a Bachelor of Arts (B.A.), Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.), or Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com.) degree from a recognized university.
Minimum required overall aggregate: 50% marks in the qualifying undergraduate examination.
Equivalency qualifications approved by the University and State Government rules are also accepted.
##### Admission Process
Admissions are strictly governed by university norms and state government policies:
Merit-Based Selection: Admissions are awarded based on aggregate marks obtained in the qualifying degree exam or state entrance tests.
Document Verification: Shortlisted candidates submit original academic transcripts and eligibility documents.
Seat Confirmation: Final enrollment follows fee payment within the stipulated university timeline.
Note: Admissions are strictly governed by university norms and state government policies. Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs and reservation details.
# Career Pathways & Opportunities
Professional avenues open to B.Ed. graduates across school education, administration, and EdTech
School Teaching
Teach in public, private, CBSE, ICSE, and State Board secondary/higher secondary schools as Trained Graduate Teachers (TGT).
Educational Leadership
Step into administrative roles such as Academic Coordinators, Headmasters/Headmistresses, and Vice-Principals.
Higher Studies
Pursue Master of Education to qualify for Assistant Professorships in teacher education colleges or doctoral research in education.
Curriculum & Content
Curriculum design, textbook writing, and educational content development
EdTech & Training
Educational technology, corporate training, and online teaching
Government Services
Teaching positions through state and central recruitment exams
