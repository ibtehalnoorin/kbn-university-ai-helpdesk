# B.Sc. in Optometry
###### Course Duration : 4 Years
##### Salient features
Bachelor of Optometry path or B.Opto is designed to impart information related to eyes, imaginative and prescient and optics. The direction accommodates three years of schooling which will offer students with realistic know-how on this subject. Through this course, students learn about the different processes that comprise the vision and optical systems. It also contains statistics that is essential to understand the producing and manufacturing of optical useful resource which include touch lenses, spectacles, and so forth. Those who efficiently whole this course are eligible to diagnose diseases related to eyes and imaginative and prescient.
