The Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) programme at Khaja Bandanawaz University is structured to provide rigorous theoretical understanding alongside intensive practical training across foundational science disciplines.
Designed under modern academic guidelines, the program empowers students with analytical skills, experimental methodology, scientific enquiry, and interdisciplinary competence across Life Sciences, Physical Sciences, Chemical Sciences, and Nutrition Sciences.
##### Eligibility Criteria
Successful completion of 10+2 / Pre-University Course (PUC) / Senior Secondary Examination from a recognised board.
Science stream preferred, with relevant subjects as per the chosen combination (e.g., Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics / Biology).
With a minimum of 55% marks in aggregate
Note: Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs, reservation details, and application timelines.

# Academic Infrastructure & Facilities
Equipped with state-of-the-art laboratory and learning resources
Advanced Science Labs
Fully equipped laboratories for Chemistry, Botany, Zoology, Physics, and Molecular Biotechnology with modern instrumentation.
Computer & Computational Lab
High-speed computer laboratories featuring programming tools, data processing software, and digital learning assets.
Central & Departmental Library
Extensive repository of science textbooks, international research journals, e-books, and scientific databases.
Research & Seminar Culture
Regular guest lectures, field trips, scientific exhibitions, workshops, and project-based practical assignments.
# Career Pathways & Graduate Scope
Multiple professional avenues across research, industry, health, and higher studies
Higher Education (M.Sc. / Ph.D.)
Eligible for M.Sc. in Chemistry, Physics, Biotechnology, Zoology, Botany, Food Science, and Data Science across national and international universities.
IT & Data Analysis
Graduates with Computer Science combinations can enter software engineering, data analytics, web development, and IT consultancy.
R&D and Laboratory Research
Opportunities as Research Assistants, Lab Analysts, and Quality Control Officers in pharmaceutical, chemical, and biotech firms.
Civil Services & Public Sector
Eligible for State and Central Competitive Exams (UPSC, KPSC, Banking, Scientific Officer posts in government bodies).
Food & Healthcare Industry
Career roles as Nutritionists, Dietitians, Food Quality Inspectors, and Healthcare Consultants in hospitals and food processing companies.
Teaching & Academics
Pursue B.Ed. or M.Sc. to become science educators in secondary, higher secondary, and degree institutes.
