The 5-Year Integrated Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Laws (B.A. LL.B.) at Khaja Bandanawaz University is crafted for students entering legal studies directly after Higher Secondary Education (10+2). It integrates foundational humanities (Political Science, Sociology, Economics, and History) with comprehensive legal disciplines.
By starting legal coursework in year one, candidates gain early exposure to case law, legal reasoning, advocacy skills, and institutional frameworks over 10 active academic semesters.
Courtroom Moot Advocacy
Integrated Arts & Legal Pedagogy
Constitutional & Administrative Law
Judicial & Corporate Internship Tracks
##### Eligibility Criteria
Candidates applying for direct admission to the 5-Year B.A. LL.B. integrated program must satisfy the following criteria:
Successful completion of 10+2 / Pre-University Course (PUC) / Senior Secondary Examination from a recognized State/Central Board.
Minimum aggregate score requirement: 45% total marks in the qualifying examination.
Admissions follow merit metrics according to University and Bar Council of India guidelines.
Note: Admissions are governed by university norms and BCI guidelines. Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs and reservation details.
