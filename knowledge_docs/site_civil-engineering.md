Civil Engineering is a field of study that involves the design, construction, and maintenance of infrastructure and buildings. This branch of engineering focuses on developing the knowledge and skills to design and build structures that are safe, sustainable, and efficient.
The course covers core principles including mathematics, physics, geology, and materials science, along with specialised topics such as structural engineering, transportation engineering, water resource engineering, environmental engineering, and construction management.
Students learn about materials used in construction (concrete, steel, timber), design principles of bridges, buildings, dams and roads, and the importance of safety and environmental sustainability. Practical learning includes laboratory work, site visits, internships, and use of advanced software tools
##### Eligibility
PUC / Intermediate from any state or recognised board, or 12th Class from any recognised board, with a minimum of 45% marks in aggregate.
Should have studied Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (or Computer Science / Biology / Electronics / Allied subjects) at this level.
Having cleared the Entrance test held by State Government (Govt. Quota) and KRLMPCA (management quota).

# Career Pathways & Opportunities
Diverse career paths in public and private sectors for civil engineering graduates
Structural Design Engineer
Design residential high-rises, commercial developments, and industrial plants using CAD and FEA structural simulation tools.
R&D and Higher Studies
Pursue M.Tech / Ph.D. research in sustainable materials, computational structures, or qualify GATE for PSU recruitments.
Construction & Project Management
Supervise large-scale infrastructure sites, metro lines, bridges, and commercial projects with cost and scheduling controls.
Academician
Teaching and research careers in civil engineering disciplines.
Government & Public Works
Qualify for public engineering cadres in PWD, CPWD, Water Resources, Irrigation Departments, and Indian Railways.
Transportation & Geotechnical Specialist
Provide specialized soil foundation testing, highway pavement design, and urban transit corridor engineering.
