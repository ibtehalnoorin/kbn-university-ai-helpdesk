Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) is a branch of engineering that deals with the study of computers and computational systems, including both hardware and software. It involves the design, development, and analysis of algorithms, programming languages, software systems, and computer hardware.
The Department of Computer Science and Engineering was established in 1987 and has grown to become one of the most popular engineering branches. CSE courses typically cover data structures, algorithms, operating systems, programming languages, computer architecture, databases, and software engineering.
Specialised areas include Artificial Intelligence, Computer Networks, Database Systems, Computer Architecture, and Software Engineering — preparing graduates for roles across software development, data science, cybersecurity, networking, and hardware design.
##### Eligibility
PUC / Intermediate from any state or recognised board, or 12th Class from any recognised board, with a minimum of 45% marks in aggregate.
Should have studied Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (or Computer Science / Biology / Electronics / Allied subjects) at this level.
Having cleared the Entrance test held by State Government (Govt. Quota) and KRLMPCA (management quota).
Intake: 120 seats (72 Management · 48 KEA) · Fees: Rs. 1,44,000/- per year. Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs and reservation details.
