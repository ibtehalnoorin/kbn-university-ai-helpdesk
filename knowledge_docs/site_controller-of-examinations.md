### Controller of Examinations
### Dr. Mohammed Abdul Baseer
### Controller of Examinations (I/c) and
### Deputy Controller of Examinations
### Khaja Bandanawaz University
Dr. Mohammed Abdul Baseer, currently serving as the Controller of Examinations (In-Charge) at Khaja Bandanawaz University, is a doctor by profession. He is an alumnus of Khaja Bandanawaz Institute of Medical Sciences and holds a postgraduate degree in Preventive and Social Medicine from Navodaya Medical College, Raichur.
With over 15 years of academic experience, Dr. Baseer has made significant contributions as a Professor and Head of the Department of Preventive and Social Medicine at the Faculty of Medical Sciences (FOMS). During his tenure, he has published 10 articles and actively led numerous community outreach initiatives including health camps, village health services, family adoption programs, and school health services.
In addition to his academic roles, he also marks administrative roles, Dr. M.A. Baseer has been serving as the Director of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) at Khaja Bandanawaz University for the past three years. He assumed the role of Deputy Controller of Examinations in February 2024 and is currently Serving as the Controller of Examinations (In-Charge) in Khaja Bandanawaz University.
