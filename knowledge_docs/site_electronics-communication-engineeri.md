Electronics and Communication Engineering (E&CE) is a branch of engineering that deals with the design, development, and maintenance of communication systems, electronic equipment, and devices. This field combines principles from both electrical engineering and computer science to design and develop various types of communication systems.
ECE courses typically cover circuit analysis, digital electronics, communication systems, signal processing, and microprocessors. Students also learn programming languages, software design, and computer networks.
Specialised areas include Telecommunications Engineering, Digital Signal Processing, Analog and Digital Electronics, Control Systems, and VLSI Design — preparing graduates for roles across electronics, communications, embedded systems, and related industries.
##### Eligibility
PUC / Intermediate from any state or recognised board, or 12th Class from any recognised board, with a minimum of 45% marks in aggregate.
Should have studied Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (or Computer Science / Biology / Electronics / Allied subjects) at this level.
Having cleared the Entrance test held by State Government (Govt. Quota) and KRLMPCA (management quota).

# Career Pathways & Opportunities
Explore versatile career trajectories across core electronics, semiconductor, and IT sectors
Semiconductor & VLSI Design
Work as RTL designers, physical design engineers, and verification specialists in semiconductor companies like Intel, Qualcomm, and Texas Instruments.
Software Engineering & IT Development
Transition into high-paying software engineering, data analytics, and full-stack development roles in global technology consultancies.
Embedded Systems & IoT Engineering
Develop firmware, automotive electronic control units (ECUs), smart home appliances, and medical instrumentation.
PSUs, Defense & Core Research
Qualify for public sector engineering positions at ISRO, DRDO, BEL, BHEL, and BSNL or pursue advanced M.Tech and Ph.D. studies.
Telecommunications & Wireless Networks
Design and manage 5G infrastructure, satellite communications, optical fiber backbones, and network protocols.
Robotics, Automation & AI Hardware
Engineer industrial automation controllers, robotic sensors, edge AI accelerators, and autonomous navigation hardware.
