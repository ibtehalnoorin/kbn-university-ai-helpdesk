# About the Faculty of Arts, Humanities & Social Sciences
The Faculty of Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences at Khaja Bandanawaz University is dedicated to fostering critical thinking, cultural understanding, and social awareness. It offers a range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes that explore literature, history, sociology, media, and related disciplines.
Through a blend of theoretical foundations and practical engagement, the Faculty prepares students to become thoughtful professionals, researchers, educators, and communicators who contribute meaningfully to society.
Students benefit from academic events, field visits, seminars, and community engagement activities that enrich classroom learning.
Interdisciplinary Research
Focus on regional studies, social structures, and media impact.
Civil Services Alignment
Curriculum designed to support UPSC and KPSC competitive exams.
# Vision & Mission
Vision
To be a center of academic excellence that nurtures socially conscious thinkers, impactful researchers, and empathetic leaders through interdisciplinary humanistic education.
Promoting inclusive humanistic values and social ethics.
Establishing regional and national research benchmarks.
Inspiring innovation in media, governance, and social policy
Mission
Provide quality education in humanities, social sciences, and media studies that fosters analytical and creative abilities.
Promote interdisciplinary learning, research, and community engagement.
Develop professionals equipped with ethical values, communication skills, and social awareness.
Encourage inclusive education and respect for cultural diversity.
Contribute to nation-building through knowledge, dialogue, and service.
# Dean’s Message
##### Dr. Nishat Arif Hussaini
##### Dean, Faculty of Languages, Law, Arts, Humanities & Social Sciences
Welcome to the Faculty of Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences at Khaja Bandanawaz University. Our Faculty is committed to the pursuit of knowledge that deepens understanding of society, culture, media, and the human experience.
Through programmes in Arts, Mass Communication & Journalism, Sociology, and doctoral research, we aim to equip students with critical thinking, ethical grounding, and practical skills. We believe education must not only inform the mind but also shape character and inspire service to humanity.
I invite you to join us in this journey of learning, inquiry, and meaningful engagement with the world around us.
