# About the Faculty of Commerce & Management
he Faculty of Commerce & Management at Khaja Bandanawaz University is committed to developing skilled professionals in accounting, finance, business, and management. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes designed to meet the needs of industry and entrepreneurship.
Through a blend of theoretical foundations and practical exposure — including industrial visits, projects, and professional orientation — the Faculty prepares students for careers in commerce, corporate sectors, banking, and higher research.
The programmes emphasise ethical business practices, financial literacy, and the skills required for a dynamic global economy.

# Vision & Mission
Vision
To be a centre of excellence in commerce and management education that produces competent, ethical, and innovative professionals capable of contributing to business, industry, and society — in alignment with the University’s motto of “Educating Humanity”.
Mission
Provide quality education in commerce, accounting, and management.
Foster analytical, entrepreneurial, and leadership skills among students.
Promote research, industry interface, and continuous learning.
Instil ethical values and social responsibility in business practice.
Prepare graduates for professional careers and higher studies.
