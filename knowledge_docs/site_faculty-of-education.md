# About the Faculty of Education
The Faculty of Education at Khaja Bandanawaz University is committed to excellence in teacher education, pedagogical innovation, and nation-building. Formerly known as Sayyid Akbar Husayni College of Education, it offers rigorous programs that blend theory with practical classroom experience.
Our faculty nurtures reflective practitioners who are ethically grounded, technologically proficient, and socially responsible educators — prepared to shape the future of learning in diverse educational settings.
With a strong emphasis on NCTE norms, inclusive education, and continuous professional development, we strive to produce teachers who inspire, lead, and transform communities.
Practical Pedagogy
Real classroom teaching practice in partner schools, ensuring graduates are classroom-ready.
Digital Skills
Integration of modern educational technologies and digital pedagogy into teaching practice.
# Vision & Mission
Vision
To be recognized as a premier center of teacher education and educational research that inspires, leads, and transforms communities through ethical, inclusive, and innovative pedagogical practices.
Mission
Deliver NCTE-compliant teacher training programs that instill deep pedagogical and psychological insights.
Incorporate digital technologies and modern educational tools into teacher preparation.
Promote research in curriculum development, policy evaluation, and inclusive learning systems.
Instill values of equity, social responsibility, and lifelong learning in future educators.

# Academic Programs
Designed to meet national NCTE guidelines and equip graduates with
essential teaching competencies.
Undergraduate
Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.)
A flagship two-year professional degree preparing secondary school educators with pedagogical skills and subject-specific methodologies.
Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
Annual Intake: 50 Seats
Eligibility: Bachelor's Degree with 50%
[Explore B.Ed Program](https://www.kbn.university/b-ed)
Doctoral
Ph.D. in Education
A research-intensive doctoral program fostering innovation in teaching methods, policy evaluation, and educational theory.
Duration: 3-5 Years
Focus: Advanced Research
Eligibility: Master's Degree in Education
