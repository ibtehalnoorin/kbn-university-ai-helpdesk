# About the Faculty of Engineering & Technology
The Faculty of Engineering and Technology at Khaja Bandanawaz University offers undergraduate (B.E.), postgraduate (M.Tech.), and doctoral (Ph.D.) programmes across core and emerging engineering disciplines.
The Faculty emphasises laboratory training, industry interaction, projects, and professional development. It is among the early institutes in Karnataka to offer Aeronautical Engineering, and maintains active engagement through workshops, industrial visits, and techno-cultural events.
Programmes are aligned with AICTE norms, and the Faculty provides information and compliance details as required by regulatory bodies.
# Vision & Mission
Vision
To evolve into a globally recognized center of excellence in engineering and technological education, research, and innovation, empowering graduates to address complex global challenges and lead industrial advancement.
Mission
Provide high-quality technical education integrated with hands-on practical skills and industry exposure.
Foster interdisciplinary research, innovation, and technological entrepreneurship.
Establish strategic partnerships with leading industries and international institutions.
Instill ethical leadership, professional integrity, and environmental responsibility among engineers.
