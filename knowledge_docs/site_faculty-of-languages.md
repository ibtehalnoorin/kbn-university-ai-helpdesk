# About the Faculty of Languages
The Faculty of Languages at Khaja Bandanawaz University is dedicated to the study of language, literature, and culture. It offers postgraduate and research programmes in English, Urdu, Hindi, and Arabic, fostering linguistic competence, critical reading, and literary appreciation.
Through rigorous academic training and engagement with classical and contemporary texts, the Faculty prepares students for careers in teaching, research, translation, media, and cultural institutions.
The Faculty also offers a Diploma in Arabic and maintains an active presence through events, podcasts, and community engagement that celebrate linguistic diversity.
# Vision & Mission
Vision
To emerge as a premier center of excellence in language studies, literature, and translation, fostering global communication, intellectual inquiry, and intercultural harmony through academic rigor.
Mission
Deliver comprehensive academic programs that instill deep critical and creative thinking.
Promote interdisciplinary research in comparative literature and translation studies.
Equip students with modern communication skills for careers in media, translation, and academia.
Preserve and celebrate classical literary traditions while adapting to contemporary linguistic trends.
