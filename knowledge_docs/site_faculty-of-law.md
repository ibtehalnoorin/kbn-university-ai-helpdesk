# About the Faculty of Law
The Faculty of Law at Khaja Bandanawaz University is dedicated to nurturing ethical, skilled, and socially conscious legal professionals. We provide a rigorous academic environment that combines strong theoretical foundations with practical exposure to the justice system.
Our programs emphasize critical thinking, advocacy skills, legal research, and a deep understanding of constitutional values, human rights, and contemporary legal challenges. Students are prepared to serve as advocates, judicial officers, corporate counsel, and policy makers.
With a commitment to excellence, integrity, and access to justice, the Faculty of Law strives to contribute meaningfully to the rule of law and nation-building.
Moot Court Exercises
Simulated courtroom sessions to build advocacy confidence and courtroom skills.
Legal Aid Clinic
Free community legal assistance and rights awareness for society at large.
# Vision & Mission
Vision
To emerge as a center of legal scholarship, constitutional advocacy, and social justice that nurtures ethically grounded, socially responsible, and globally competent legal professionals.
Mission
Deliver BCI-compliant legal education integrating statutory theory with practical advocacy skills.
Inculcate constitutional values, professional ethics, and a commitment to access to justice.
Promote legal research in emerging domains including cyber law, corporate governance, and human rights.
Provide community legal services through legal literacy camps and free legal aid clinics.

# Academic Law Programs
Regulated by the Bar Council of India to prepare students for judicial, corporate, and litigation paths.
Graduate Law Program
LL.B. (Bachelor of Laws)
A professional degree program for graduates in any academic discipline seeking to enter legal practice, advocacy, or corporate advisory.
Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
Eligibility: Bachelor's Degree with min 45%
[View LL.B. Details](https://www.kbn.university/llb)
Integrated Dual Degree
B.A. LL.B. (Integrated)
Designed for students entering directly after 10+2 / PUC. Combines Arts & Humanities foundation with core legal disciplines over 10 semesters.
Duration: 5 Years (10 Semesters)
Eligibility: 10+2 / PUC with min 45%
[View B.A. LL.B. Details](https://www.kbn.university/ballb)
# Career Pathways for Law Graduates
Graduates from KBN University Faculty of Law transition into diverse legal roles.
Legal Practice & Advocacy
Enroll with Bar Councils to practice in District, High, or Supreme Courts.
Judicial Services
Prepare for Civil Judge and Judicial Service competitive exams.
Corporate Law Counsel
Advisory and compliance roles across corporate legal firms.
Public Service & NGOs
Policy analysis, legal advocacy, and human rights protection.
