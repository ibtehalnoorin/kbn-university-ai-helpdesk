# About the Faculty of Sciences
The Faculty of Sciences at Khaja Bandanawaz University is dedicated to advancing knowledge in the natural and physical sciences. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes across biological sciences, physical sciences, mathematics, and applied fields such as biotechnology and food & nutrition.
Through laboratory training, field exposure, seminars, and research-oriented learning, the Faculty prepares students for careers in research, industry, healthcare, education, and higher studies.
The Faculty actively organises conferences, workshops, and educational visits that connect classroom learning with contemporary scientific practice.
# Vision & Mission
Vision
To be recognized globally as a premier hub for scientific education and interdisciplinary research, empowering students to solve real-world challenges through innovation, ethical inquiry, and scientific advancement.
Mission
Deliver quality education across foundational and modern scientific disciplines.
Promote innovative research through modern laboratory infrastructure and academic collaborations.
Cultivate problem-solving and analytical capabilities to meet industrial and societal needs.
Inculcate professional ethics, environmental sustainability, and a passion for continuous learning.
