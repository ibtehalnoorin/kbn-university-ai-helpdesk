### Finance Officer
### Abkar S. Bakash
### Finance Officer (I/c)
### Khaja Bandanawaz University
With immense gratitude and humility, I wish to share my journey and ongoing commitment in serving the noble cause of education under the esteemed umbrella of Khaja Education Society and Khaja Bandanawaz University.
Prior to joining this institution, I was actively involved in CA and CS coaching classes and served as a guest lecturer for postgraduate courses in various reputed colleges in Hubli. In the year 2017, I was blessed with a significant opportunity to serve Khaja Education Society as Chief Accounts Officer, a moment of honor made possible by the spiritual guidance and blessings of His Holiness Syed Shah Khusro Hussaini (Rahmatullahi Alaihi), the former President of the Society.
Since then, I have had the privilege to work under the visionary leadership of Janab Syed Muhammad Ali Al Hussaini Sahab, the current President of Khaja Education Society and Hon’ble Chancellor of Khaja Bandanawaz University. His constant guidance, trust, and unwavering support have been the driving force behind every development in the finance and accounts domain of our institution.
In 2018, I was entrusted with the additional responsibility as In-Charge Finance Officer of Khaja Bandanawaz University, right from its inception. Over these years, our accounts and finance team has consistently worked towards building a transparent, efficient, and accountable financial system within the University. Some of the major milestones we collectively achieved include:
Establishing robust accounting and reporting systems across all constituent institutions
Ensuring full compliance with all statutory obligations, including PF, ESI, GST, TDS, and Income Tax matters
Strengthening relationships with leading public and private sector banks to facilitate institutional growth and funding
Streamlining financial transactions with appropriate internal controls and periodic audits
Overseeing proper documentation and legal processes concerning institutional properties and capital assets
Managing revenue matters diligently with timely projections and budgeting practices
Throughout my career, I have upheld the principles of honesty, integrity, and accountability, without compromise under any circumstance. The success we have achieved in the finance section is not due to individual effort alone but is a reflection of the collective dedication of our finance team, driven by the inspirational leadership of Syed Muhammad Ali Al Hussaini Sahab, Hon’ble Chancellor of KBNU.
I express my sincere thanks to the management, academic heads, faculty, and administrative staff who continuously support our financial discipline and development goals. I remain committed to serving the university with sincerity and professionalism and to contribute positively to its future growth and financial stability.
