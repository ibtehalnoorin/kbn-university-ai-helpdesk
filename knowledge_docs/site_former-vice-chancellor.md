##### Former Vice Chancellor
## Prof. A.M. Pathan
First Vice Chancellor
28-08-2018 to 20-06-2022
The Khaja Bandanawaz University is a young institution, has been established on 23rd August 2018 by the Karnataka State Government Legislation Act (No.18 of 2018).
It has been founded by the Chancellor Janab Dr. Syed Shah Khusro Hussaini whose resolute determination has seen the realization of the vision of his father Alhaj Padma Shree Hazrath Syed Shah Muhammad Muhammad Al-Hussaini, who was the visionary Founder of the Khaja Education Society in 1958.
The University is poised for dynamic growth with the aim of providing quality education to the youth of the region. The aim is to promote intellectual growth among the student and to encourage research and learning. We bear the name and vision of the Sufi Saint, Philosopher and Teacher Hazrath Khaja Bandanawaz Gisudaraz who encouraged learning, tolerance and brotherhood.
The Khaja Bandanawaz University is situated in a region of historic and cultural relevance and Kalaburagi has been home to Saints, Philosophers and Thinkers. We look forward to the active contribution of all the aspirants, Students and Teachers towards this great vision of academic excellence of the University.
