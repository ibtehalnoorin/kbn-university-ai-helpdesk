# Why Choose Khaja Bandanawaz University?
Providing unparalleled infrastructure, industry accreditation, and career pathways.
NMC & AICTE Approvals
Accredited and approved by statutory regulatory bodies ensuring valid national and international degree recognition.
Advanced Research Labs
Dedicated research centers in Biotechnology, Molecular Diagnostics, and Advanced Engineering Prototyping.
1500+ Bed Teaching Hospital
Khaja Banda Nawaz Teaching Hospital provides continuous hands-on clinical exposure for medical and nursing students.
Infosys Springboard Partnership
