The Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) program at Khaja Bandanawaz University is a 3-year full-time professional degree intended for graduates seeking a career in the legal field. Legal education in India is regulated by the statutory Bar Council of India (BCI) under the Advocates Act, 1961.
The curriculum combines rigorous theoretical study with practical legal training, courtroom moot exercises, legal aid clinics, and internships.
Moot Court & Advocacy Practice
Legal Aid & Clinical Education
Constitutional & Corporate Law
Legal Drafting & Dispute Resolution
##### Eligibility Criteria
Applicants seeking admission to the 3-Year LL.B. program must fulfill the following qualification requirements:
Candidates must hold a Bachelor’s degree in any discipline (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com, or equivalent) from a UGC-recognized university.
Minimum aggregate requirement: 45% total marks in the qualifying undergraduate degree.
Selection follows merit and policies outlined by the State Government and University guidelines.
# Focus Areas
Core components of the LL.B. programme
Moot Court & Advocacy
Simulated courtroom sessions to build advocacy confidence and courtroom skills.
Community legal assistance and hands-on clinical training.
Strong foundation in constitutional values and corporate legal practice.
Drafting skills and methods of resolving legal disputes effectively.
