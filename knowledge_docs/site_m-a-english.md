Under the Faculty of Languages, Khaja Bandanawaz University has well-established Departments of English and Urdu, offering students the opportunity to pursue postgraduate and doctoral research in their chosen discipline.
The Department of English is a well-established unit with a reputation for excellent standards and discipline built over the past ten years. Student strength is optimal, and many applicants are turned away. Students have regularly secured ranks and distinction in English.
Under KBN University, the Department has strengthened its distinctive quality through a versatile and rich selection of courses designed in consultation with leading subject experts. The M.A. English programme is a modern international course that blends pure literature, classic and modern texts, history of literature, modern literary theory, critical studies, cultural and gender theories, novel, prose, and poetry. European literature and thought, including French and German philosophers, are also studied for their influence on English writers.
# Focus Areas
Core components of the M.A. English programme
Literature & Texts
Pure literature, classic and modern texts, novel, prose, and poetry.
Cultural & Gender Studies
Cultural theories, gender theories, and interdisciplinary perspectives.
History of Literature
Historical development of English literature across periods and movements.
European Thought
European literature and influence of French and German philosophers on English writers.
Literary Theory & Criticism
Modern literary theory, critical studies, and interpretive frameworks.
# Academic Environment & Resources
Providing students with scholarly resources, research guidance, and language tools
Departmental & Central Library
Extensive repository of rare literary works, international research journals, critical guides, and digital archives.
Language & Phonetics Lab
Advanced audio-visual lab for accent training, phonetics practice, listening skills, and presentation mastery.
Literary Club & Debating Society
Student-led forums organizing book reviews, poetry recitations, national seminars, drama workshops, and debates.
NET / SET Guidance Cell
Special mentorship and coaching sessions conducted by expert faculty to help students clear national eligibility tests.
##### Eligibility Criteria
Eligibility: B.A. from any recognized university with a minimum 55% marks in aggregate and should have studied English as one of the optional subjects at the undergraduate level
Admission is generally based on Merit-based admission as per University norms.
Note: Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs, reservation details, and application timelines.
# Career Pathways & Opportunities
Dynamic professional options for postgraduates in English literature and critical studies
Higher Education & Professorship
Qualify UGC-NET/SET to become Assistant Professors, Lecturers, and Academic Researchers in universities and colleges.
Publishing, Editing & Journalism
Content editors, copywriters, publishing house managers, print/digital journalists, and literary critics.
Corporate Communication & PR
Public Relations officers, corporate communications managers, brand strategists, and speechwriters in MNCs.
Civil Services & Public Policy
Success in UPSC and State Public Service examinations with English Literature as an optional subject.
Translation & Technical Writing
Language specialists, technical writers, creative content strategists, and cross-cultural translators.
Doctoral Research (Ph.D.)
Direct entry into Ph.D. in English at KBNU or global universities in specialized literature and linguistic domains.
