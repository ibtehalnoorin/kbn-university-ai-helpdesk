A Master of Arts (M.A.) in Mass Communication and Journalism is a postgraduate degree that provides advanced training in mass media and communication. The programme develops analytical, critical thinking, and practical skills needed for careers in journalism, public relations, advertising, and related fields.
It combines coursework, research, and hands-on training. Students study advanced media theory and research along with specialized areas such as broadcast journalism, print journalism, public relations, advertising, and digital media.
The Department provides practical training in Media and Communication. Students also get exposure through national-level sports events hosted by KBN House of Sports and print journalism experience via KBN Times.
# Specialized Areas
Tailor your postgraduate focus toward specialized sectors of the media and communication industry.
Investigative Journalism
Focuses on in-depth research, ethics, and investigative reporting on complex societal issues.
Media Management
Managing operations, personnel, content distribution, and budgeting within media firms.
Multimedia & Digital Media
Content creation across digital formats, web platforms, podcasts, and social media production.
Digital Media Production
Content creation for websites, social media, mobile apps, and digital platforms.
Strategic Communication & PR
Developing public relations campaigns, corporate messaging, and strategic marketing communications.
Broadcast & Print
Training in television, radio, and print journalism practices and storytelling.
Hands-on Training
Practical exposure in media production, reporting, and communication skills through departmental facilities.
Live Event Coverage
National & international sports events at KBN House of Sports provide real-time journalism experience.
KBN Times
In-house print journalism platform giving students opportunities to write, edit and publish.
##### Eligibility Criteria
### Basic Eligibility
Bachelor’s degree in any stream from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate.
Candidates from any discipline (Arts, Science, Commerce, or professional degrees) are eligible to apply.
Note: Admission is based on merit in the qualifying examination and as per University guidelines. Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs and reservation details.
# Career Pathways & Opportunities
Graduates transition directly into roles in newsrooms, PR agencies, and digital media houses:
Journalism & Newsrooms
Reporters, desk editors, anchors, and news producers in print, TV, and digital outlets.
Public Relations & PR
Corporate communication specialists, media relations managers, and PR strategists.
Advertising & Creative
Copywriters, brand managers, and creative directors in ad agencies.
Media managers and executives in media organizations
Digital Media & Social
Social media managers, digital video producers, and content strategists.
