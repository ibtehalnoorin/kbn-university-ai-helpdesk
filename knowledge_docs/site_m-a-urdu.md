The M.A. in Urdu course covers every aspect of literature — from the history of Urdu literature and classic texts to modern writers; from prose and poetry to novels, short stories, criticism, research methodology, and grammar.
Special importance is given to the Urdu language and literature and its development through the phases of history. The syllabus is divided into four semesters.
The programme provides career opportunities for candidates in Urdu-knowing business communities and in teaching, media, translation, and related fields.
# Focus Areas
Core components of the M.A. Urdu programme
History of Urdu Literature
Development of Urdu language and literature through historical phases.
Criticism & Research
Literary criticism, research methodology, and scholarly approaches.
Classic & Modern Texts
Classic texts and modern writers across prose, poetry, novels, and short stories.
Grammar & Language
Urdu grammar and language skills for academic and professional use.
Prose & Poetry
In-depth study of Urdu prose and poetic traditions and forms.
# Academic Environment & Resources
Providing students with scholarly resources, research guidance, and language tools
Deccani Manuscript & Reference Library
Access to rare classical manuscripts, historical archives, critical journals, and a specialized collection of Deccani literature.
Urdu Literary Association (Anjuman-e-Adab)
Student body hosting regular mushairas, literary seminars, essay competitions, book launches, and guest lectures by eminent scholars.
Urdu DTP & Language Technology Suite
Computers equipped with InPage Urdu DTP, Unicode fonts, and translation tools for digital media and publication publishing.
NET / SET & Competitive Exam Guidance
Special mentorship programs conducted by experienced faculty to assist postgraduates in clearing UGC-NET, SLET, and eligibility tests.
##### Eligibility Criteria
Eligibility: B.A. from any recognized university with a minimum 55% marks in aggregate and should have studied English as one of the optional subjects at the undergraduate level
Admission is generally based on Merit-based admission as per University norms.
Note: Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs, reservation details, and application timelines.
# Career Pathways & Opportunities
Dynamic professional options for postgraduates in English literature and critical studies
Academic Professorship & Teaching
Clear UGC-NET/SET to work as Assistant Professors, Lecturers, and School Educators in government and private institutions.
Urdu Journalism & Mass Media
Roles as news editors, columnists, print/TV journalists, copywriters, and content curators in national Urdu media organizations.
Translation & Language Expertise
Professional translators and interpreters for publishing houses, government academies (Urdu Academies), and cultural bodies.
Civil Services & Public Administration
Targeting UPSC and State Public Service examinations with Urdu Literature as an optional subject.
Content Creation & Copywriting
Scriptwriting for television, radio, theater, film, digital portals, and creative content strategy in Urdu.
Doctoral Research (Ph.D.)
Direct entry into doctoral research programs focusing on Deccani manuscripts, modern criticism, and textual editing.
