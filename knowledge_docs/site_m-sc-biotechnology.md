Khaja Bandanawaz University offers B.Sc. and M.Sc. Biotechnology courses with advanced facilities. The international-standard course structure has been developed in consultation with clinical, agricultural and industrial experts to provide cutting-edge knowledge and practical skills for a career in molecular biosciences.
Students gain extensive subject knowledge and research experience through research-based teaching in well-established biotechnology laboratories, hands-on training in Bioinformatics, internships in industry or medical institutions, and a full-semester research project under the mentorship of highly qualified faculty recruited from reputed universities across the country.
The programme follows a Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), allowing students to opt for courses of their choice from the pool of Biotechnology courses. The University is a pioneer in introducing international patterns of examination — Library Based, Take Home and Seminar Based Examinations — alongside traditional written exams, enhancing quality of learning and research-oriented thinking with reduced examination stress. The aim is to develop a sustainable pipeline of biotechnology professionals across fundamental science, technology development and translational research.
##### Eligibility Criteria
Candidates seeking admission into M.Sc. Biotechnology must fulfill the following criteria:
Passed B.Sc. in Biotechnology / Biochemistry / Life Sciences / Microbiology / Chemistry / Agriculture or relevant discipline from a recognized university.
Secured at least 55% aggregate marks (or equivalent CGPA) in the qualifying undergraduate degree.
Admissions are processed as per University guidelines and merit criteria.
Note: Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs, reservation details, and application timelines.
# Specialized Areas & Thrust Research Domains
Explore specialized elective tracks and advanced research domains within the postgraduate curriculum
Recombinant DNA Technology & Genomics
Gene cloning vector construction, PCR amplification, CRISPR-Cas gene editing, nucleic acid sequencing, and genomic library design.
Animal Cell Culture & Tissue Engineering
Primary cell culture, cell line maintenance, stem cell biology, cytotoxicity testing, and tissue scaffold culture systems.
Biopharmaceutical & Medical Biotechnology
Monoclonal antibody development, vaccine design, therapeutic proteins, molecular diagnostics, and bioprocess purification.
Industrial Fermentation & Bioprocess Tech
Bioreactor design, upstream fermentations, enzyme engineering, downstream recovery, and scale-up economics.
Plant & Agricultural Biotechnology
Micropropagation tissue culture, transgenic crop generation, marker-assisted selection, stress tolerance, and biofertilizers.
Bioinformatics & Computational Biology
Sequence alignment algorithms, molecular docking, structural biology modeling, genomic databases, and Next-Gen Sequencing data.
# Departmental Infrastructure & Exposure
Modern practical equipment, research mentorship, and experiential field tours
Molecular Biology & PCR Suite
Thermal cyclers (PCR), gel electrophoresis units, UV transilluminators, gel documentation systems, and refrigerated centrifuges.
Tissue Culture & Sterile Workstation
Laminar air flow cabinets, CO2 incubators, inverted microscopes, and sterile media preparation facilities.
Bioinformatics & Computational Lab
High-performance computer workstations equipped with molecular modeling software, docking suites, and sequence alignment tools.
Central Library & E-Journals
Access to international biotechnology journals, research databases, digital archives, and specialized reference volumes.
# Career Pathways & Industry Scope
Promising careers across biopharma, agro-biotech, clinical diagnostics, research institutes, and IP management
Biotech & Pharma R&D Scientist
Roles in molecular research, drug discovery, biological assay development, and product testing in pharmaceutical companies.
Agricultural & Environmental Biotech
Work on transgenic crops, biofertilizers, seed technology, bioremediation, and industrial effluent treatment.
Bioprocess & Fermentation Engineer
Upstream and downstream production specialists managing bioreactors, enzyme manufacturing, and biological scale-up.
Intellectual Property (IPR) & Bio-Patenting
Specialists in patent filing, biological technology transfer, regulatory affairs, and bioethics management.
Quality Control & Assurance (QC/QA)
Ensuring compliance with GMP, ISO, and FDA regulatory standards in clinical, diagnostic, and biomanufacturing plants.
Doctoral Research (Ph.D.) & Academia
Pursue doctoral research or qualify CSIR-NET / GATE for academic careers in premier universities and research centers.
