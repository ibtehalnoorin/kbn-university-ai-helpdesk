# M.Sc. Medical Microbiology
###### Course Duration :
Anchor 1
M.Sc. Medical Microbiology route covers the molecular and medical factors of virology, bacteriology, immunology, and mycology to contamination. The major goal of this route is to offer the clinical basis of modern microbiological and traditional principles. In this path, applicants will develop the sensible abilities, know-how, and critical cognizance required to pursue their profession in medical microbiology.
In this path, students look at most important subjects include ideas of microbiology, research techniques, information contamination, microbial pathogenesis, global fitness and epidemiology, and molecular diagnostics. In addition to this direction, college students additionally take a look at diverse clinical programs of microbes for the development of fitness.
##### Eligibility Criteria
MBBS / BDS / BAMS / BHMS / BUMS / B.Pharma / BVSc / B.Sc. Life Sciences / B.Sc. Biology /B.Sc. Biochemistry / B.Sc. Nursing / BMLT / BPT / B.Tech. (Bio-Tech.) with minimum 55% marks.
