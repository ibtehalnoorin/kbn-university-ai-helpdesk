The M.Sc. in Zoology program at Khaja Bandanawaz University features a modern, comprehensive syllabus reviewed by experts from premier national institutes. The curriculum balances core animal biology with cutting-edge advancements in cell and molecular biology, genetics, immunology, physiology, and applied zoology.
Under a Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), students gain theoretical depth and practical experience using ICT tools, specialized laboratory equipment, hands-on field trips to zoological hotspots across India, and mentored dissertation projects. The program aims to foster sustainable scientific professionals and prospective entrepreneurs in applied animal sciences.
##### Eligibility Criteria
Candidates applying for admission must satisfy the following conditions:
Passed B.Sc. Degree in Zoology or Allied Biological / Medical Sciences from a recognized university.
Secured at least 55% aggregate marks (or equivalent CGPA) in the qualifying undergraduate degree.
Admissions are processed according to University merit guidelines and regulations.
Note: Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs, reservation details, and application timelines.
# Specialized Areas & Thrust Research Domains
Explore specialized elective tracks and advanced research domains within the postgraduate curriculum
Molecular Biology & Genetics
Focuses on advanced gene regulation, recombinant DNA technology, genomics, protein interactions, cellular signaling pathways, and modern biotechnology techniques.
Environmental Biology & Ecotoxicology
Studies ecosystem dynamics, bio-monitoring of aquatic & terrestrial ecosystems, pollutant biomarkers, toxicity mechanisms, and wildlife conservation policies.
Applied Entomology & Pest Control
In-depth study of insect physiology, vector-borne diseases, economic entomology, biological pest control strategies, and agricultural pest management systems.
Parasitology & Vector Biology
Covers parasite-host interactions, life cycles, epidemiology of zoonotic pathogens, disease vector management, and immunological defense mechanisms.
Animal Physiology & Endocrinology
Investigates organ systems, metabolic integration, hormonal controls, neuro-endocrine mechanisms, and mammalian adaptation under environmental stressors.
Hydrobiology & Aquaculture
Explores freshwater and marine ecosystem biology, commercial fish breeding, water quality analysis, nutrition, and bio-entrepreneurial aquaculture systems.
# Departmental Infrastructure & Exposure
Modern practical equipment, research mentorship, and experiential field tours
Advanced Zoology Laboratories
Outfitted with binocular & compound microscopes, spectrophotometers, centrifuges, gel electrophoresis, and dissection facilities.
Annual Experiential Field Tours
Mandatory educational visits to biodiversity hotspots, botanical/zoological reserves, marine centers, and research stations.
ICT & Digital Learning Resources
Interactive classrooms equipped with ICT tools, bio-statistical tools, computer terminals, and online e-journal subscriptions.
Central & Departmental Library
Access to thousands of volumes, classical zoological monographs, international research journals, and digital databases.
# Career Pathways & Opportunities
Diverse career prospects in scientific research, wildlife conservation, academics, and industries
Scientific Research Officer
Join national research bodies such as CSIR, ICMR, DBT, DRDO, or ICAR as Junior Research Fellows (JRF) and scientists.
Biotech & Pharmaceutical Industry
Work as Quality Control Analysts, Toxicologists, Clinical Research Associates, and R&D Officers in pharma firms.
Wildlife & Forest Conservator
Roles in forest departments, wildlife institutes, biological parks, and environmental non-governmental organizations (NGOs).
Applied Zoology & Bio-Entrepreneurship
Establish ventures in commercial aquaculture, apiculture, sericulture, vermicomposting, and pest control consultancy.
Academic Professorship
Qualify CSIR-NET / SET / GATE exams to become Assistant Professors and lecturers in colleges and universities.
Doctoral Studies (Ph.D.)
Pursue Ph.D. in specialized fields such as Molecular Biology, Neurobiology, Marine Biology, or Entomology at KBNU or top global institutes.
