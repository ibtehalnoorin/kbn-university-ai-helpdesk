M.A Arabic is a two year degree course in Arabic Language and Literature. The course will equip the student with speaking and writing skill in the Arabic language. Apart from the study of selected literary texts, the course also introduces the student to translation, from Arabic to English and vice versa.
##### Eligibility
The minimum eligibility for applying for MA Arabic admission is a minimum of 50% aggregate or a Bachelor’s degree in Arabic.
