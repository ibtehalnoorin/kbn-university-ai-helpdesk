A Master of Arts (M.A.) in Economics is a postgraduate programme that provides advanced training in economic theory, quantitative methods, and applied economics. Students develop the analytical tools needed to understand markets, public policy, development, and contemporary economic challenges.
The curriculum typically covers microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, development economics, public finance, and specialised electives. Emphasis is placed on both theoretical foundations and empirical analysis using data and statistical techniques.
Graduates are prepared for careers in research, banking, financial services, government, international organisations, consulting, and academia. The programme also serves as a strong foundation for doctoral studies in Economics.
# Specialized Areas
Core themes and specialised topics covered in the programme
Micro & Macro Theory
In-depth study of market mechanisms, consumer behavior, national income determination, inflation, and monetary policies.
Public Finance
Taxation, public expenditure, fiscal federalism, and government budgets.
Econometrics & Statistics
Quantitative analytical models, statistical techniques, and empirical data analysis for economic forecasting.
International Economics
Trade theory, balance of payments, exchange rates, and global economic institutions.
Development Economics
Evaluating growth strategies, rural poverty, sustainable development, and socio-economic transformation in developing nations.
Macroeconomics
National income, inflation, unemployment, monetary and fiscal policy.
Research Project / Dissertation
Opportunity to undertake applied economic research using data and quantitative tools.
Quantitative Skills
Training in econometrics, data analysis, and policy evaluation techniques.
Pathway to Research & Policy
Strong preparation for Ph.D., civil services, banking, and policy-oriented careers.
##### Eligibility Criteria
Bachelor’s degree in any stream from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate.
Candidates from any discipline are eligible; a background in Economics, Mathematics, or related subjects is preferred.
Admission is generally based on merit in the qualifying examination as per University norms.
Note: Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs, reservation details, and any additional requirements.
# Career Pathways & Opportunities
An M.A. in Economics provides graduates with analytical and quantitative skills that open doors across public and private sectors:
Banking & Financial
Analyst, credit, research, and roles in banks and financial institutions
Civil Services & Policy
UPSC, state services, planning, and economic policy roles
Research & Think Tanks
Economic research, data analysis, and policy institutes
Academia & Research
Lecturer, researcher, and academic positions in colleges and universities
Consulting & Corporate
Business analysis, market research, and consulting firms
