# M.A. in Geography
###### Course Duration : 2 Years
Compass Pointing North
Mountainous Landscape
Mountainous Landscape by the Sea
1/6
M.A. Geography is a two years program and the course includes a comprehensive and thorough study of the physical features of the earth and of human activity related to it. In the PG Geography Course the focus of the study is more on the social science aspect of the subject i.e. on Human Geography which is an area of study that includes the built –up environment and how space is created, viewed and managed by humans as well as the conflicts and benefits that develop from this interaction .
Duration : The course is of two years divided into four semesters.
##### Eligibility
Eligibility: Aspiring students should have passed graduation with Geography or Geography as one of the subjects studied in graduation
