M.A. in Hindi equips the aspirant with a deep study of Hindi literature and exposes students to a wide study of Hindi literature and language. The course covers interesting areas such as the novel, poetry, fiction, drama, and prose.
Hindi literature is broadly divided into four prominent forms or styles: Bhakti (devotional — Kabir, Surdas); Shringar (beauty — Keshav, Bihari); Veer-Gatha (extolling brave warriors); and Adhunik sahitya (modern literature).
The two-year programme prepares graduates for teaching, research, media, translation, and roles that require strong proficiency in Hindi language and literature.
# Focus Areas
Core components of the M.A. Hindi programme
Novel, Fiction & Drama
Study of Hindi novel, fiction, drama, and prose traditions.
Shringar & Veer-Gatha
Beauty (Keshav, Bihari) and heroic (Veer-Gatha) styles of Hindi literature.
Poetry
Hindi poetry across periods and styles, from classical to modern.
Adhunik Sahitya
Modern Hindi literature and contemporary literary trends.
Bhakti Literature
Devotional literature — Kabir, Surdas, and the Bhakti tradition.
# Academic Environment & Resources
Providing students with scholarly resources, research guidance, and language tools
Departmental & Central Library
Vast collection of classical texts, reference books, critical commentaries, national research journals, and digital archives.
Language Lab & Translation Tools
Computer suite equipped with Hindi DTP software, Devanagari typing tools, and digital translation resources.
Hindi Sahitya Parishad (Literary Club)
Platform for poetry recitations (Kavi Sammelan), essay competitions, debates, drama performances, and Hindi Diwas celebrations.
UGC-NET / SET Guidance Cell
Special mentorship and coaching sessions conducted by experienced faculty to assist postgraduates in clearing competitive tests.

# Career Pathways & Opportunities
Diverse career avenues across education, government administration, media, and translation services
Academic Professorship & Teaching
Qualify UGC-NET/SET to work as Assistant Professors, Lecturers, and Teachers in colleges and schools nationwide.
Official Language Officer (Rajbhasha Adhikari)
Prestigious roles as Hindi Officers and Senior Translation Officers in public sector banks, PSUs, and central government departments.
Hindi Media & Mass Communication
Opportunities as news readers, editors, journalists, copywriters, and scriptwriters in print, TV, and digital portals.
Translation & Content Writing
Professional roles as English-to-Hindi translators, technical writers, digital content creators, and publishing editors.
Civil Services & Public Administration
Crack State Public Service Commission (KPSC/UPSC) examinations with Hindi Literature as an optional subject.
Doctoral Research (Ph.D.)
Pursue advanced research in Hindi literature, comparative literature, and folk traditions at KBNU or top institutions.
