Political Science deals with the system of governance, political activities, analysis, ideas, thoughts and behaviours. The study of Political Science enhances knowledge of the powerful forces that shape societies and public life. Every citizen benefits from understanding the institutions and processes that govern them.
The M.A. programme trains students in different approaches to political analysis and equips them with skills essential for careers in academia, civil services, public administration, media, research, and advocacy. It covers political theory, comparative politics, international relations, Indian politics, and public administration.
Graduates develop critical thinking, analytical ability, and a deep understanding of political systems — preparing them for leadership and informed participation in public life.
# Specialized Areas
Core themes and specialised topics covered in the programme
Political Theory & Ideologies
Classical and contemporary political philosophy, Western and Indian political thought, justice, democracy, and freedom theories.
Public Administration & Governance
Principles of administrative theory, public policy implementation, administrative reforms, and digital governance.
Indian Political Thought & Constitution
Detailed study of the Indian Constitution, federalism, electoral processes, local governance, and dynamic political movements.
Comparative Politics
Political systems, institutions, and processes across countries and regions.
International Relations & Foreign Policy
Global politics, diplomatic relations, geopolitical strategy, security studies, international organizations, and foreign affairs.
Research Methods
Political analysis, qualitative and quantitative methods, and academic writing.
Research & Dissertation
Opportunity to undertake independent research on political and governance themes.
Analytical Skills
Training in critical analysis of political systems, policies, and public debates.
Pathway to Civil Services
Strong foundation for UPSC, state services, and policy-oriented careers.
##### Eligibility Criteria
Bachelor’s degree in any stream from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate.
Candidates from any discipline are eligible; a background in Political Science or related social sciences is preferred.
Admission is generally based on merit in the qualifying examination as per University norms.
Note: Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs, reservation details, and any additional requirements.
# Career Pathways & Opportunities
Graduates of M.A. Political Science possess strong analytical, analytical research, and policy-formulation capabilities applicable across multiple sectors:
Civil Services & Governance
High alignment with civil service examinations such as UPSC, KPSC, state administrative services, and municipal policy roles.
Academia & Teaching
Lecturerships, academic research, NET/SET qualification, and doctoral programs (Ph.D.).
Policy Research & Think Tanks
Roles as policy analysts, political researchers, and strategy advisors in government and non-governmental think tanks.
Political Journalism & Media
Careers in political reporting, diplomatic commentary, editorial analysis, and public affairs media.
NGOs & Advocacy
Governance, rights-based work, and civil society organisations
