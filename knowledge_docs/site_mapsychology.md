M.A. Psychology or Master of Arts in Psychology is a postgraduate Psychology course. Psychology includes a comprehensive and thorough study of Psychology as a discipline at an advanced level. M.A. (Psychology) degree course covers the study of mental processes, motives, reactions, feelings, conflict resolution, crisis management, groupthink, motivation and nature of the mind. It covers various topics like General Psychology, Social Psychology, Counseling Psychology, Health Psychology, Industrial Psychology, etc. In other words, M.A. (Psychology) degree course is a study of mental functions and human behaviour.
##### Eligibility
Bachelor’s degree in any stream from any recognized university with minimum 50% marks in it.
