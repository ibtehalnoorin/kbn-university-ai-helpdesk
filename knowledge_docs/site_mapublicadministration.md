MA in Public Administration is a two-year full time post-graduate program for the students who wish to make their career in administration of public affairs. It teaches its students how to master government administration and public services. The course gives students a basic understanding of the mechanism of various government agencies and their functioning. It also covers several socio-political issues which need to be addressed in our society. It deals mainly with financial administration and the basics of Administrative theory. The main aim of the course is to provide comprehensive knowledge on the interrelationships between State, Society and Administration. Public administration is a field in which the administrators serve communities to advance the common good and affect positive change. Public administration professionals are equipped with skills to manage at all levels of government (local, state, and federal) as well as nonprofit organizations. Besides, Public administration is one of the most popular optional subjects candidates opt for in the UPSC civil services exam. If you opt for Public Administration you will get definitely cutting edge in General Studies Mains and Essay Paper.
##### Eligibility
Bachelor’s degree in any stream from any recognized university with minimum 50% marks in it.
