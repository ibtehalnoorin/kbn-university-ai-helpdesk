A Master of Arts (M.A.) in Sociology is a graduate-level degree that focuses on the study of human social behaviour, institutions, and interactions. The programme involves coursework in sociological theory, research methods, data analysis, and specialised topics such as social stratification, gender, race, culture, globalisation, and social movements.
The goal is to provide students with the knowledge and skills needed to conduct sociological research and analyse social phenomena. Graduates are prepared for careers in academia, research institutions, government agencies, non-profit organisations, and private businesses.
In addition to coursework, students typically complete a research project or thesis involving data collection and analysis on a social phenomenon, demonstrating the ability to apply sociological theory and research methods to real-world problems.
# Key Areas of Study
Core themes and specialised topics covered in the programme
Sociological Theory
Classical and contemporary theories explaining social structure, action, and change.
Gender & Society
Gender relations, feminism, identity, and intersectionality in social life.
Research Methods
Quantitative and qualitative methods, data collection, and sociological analysis.
Culture & Globalisation
Cultural processes, identity, globalisation, and transnational social dynamics.
Social Stratification
Class, caste, inequality, mobility, and structures of advantage and disadvantage.
Social Movements
Collective action, protest, civil society, and processes of social transformation.
Research Project / Thesis
Opportunity to undertake independent research on a social issue applying theory and methods.
Field Exposure
Engagement with community, institutions, and real-world social contexts beyond the classroom.
Academic Foundation
Strong preparation for doctoral studies, teaching, and research careers in sociology.
##### Eligibility Criteria
Bachelor’s degree in any stream from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate.
Candidates from any discipline (Arts, Science, Commerce, or professional degrees) are eligible to apply.
A background in Sociology or related social sciences is preferred but not mandatory.
Note: Admission is based on merit in the qualifying examination and as per University guidelines. Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs and reservation details.
