The Faculty of Medical Sciences at Khaja Bandanawaz University is a distinguished institution renowned for its commitment to excellence in medical education. Formerly known as Khaja Bandanawaz Institute of Medical Sciences, KBNU has over 25+ years of experience in shaping the careers of aspiring medical professionals.
Recognized by the Medical Council of India / National Medical Commission, the university combines rigorous academic training with practical learning, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and a faculty of distinguished educators.
The MBBS programme is meticulously designed to provide students with a strong foundation in medical sciences, emphasising hands-on training and diagnostic proficiency. In the academic year 2021–22, intake was increased from 100 to 150 students, reflecting the institution’s growing reputation and dedication to high-quality medical education.
##### Admission & Eligibility
Admissions are governed by National Medical Commission (NMC) regulations through NEET-UG scores and official state/national counseling bodies (KEA / MCC).
Qualifying score in NEET-UG exam.
Completion of 10+2 with PCB & English subjects.
# Pre-Clinical Sciences
Establishes core foundation in human anatomy, body functions, and biochemical processes.
Human Anatomy
Dissection practicals, histology, embryology, and osteology with dedicated museum exhibits.
Physiology
Organ system functionality, hematology practicals, and clinical physiological evaluations.
Biochemistry
Metabolic pathways, molecular biology, clinical diagnostics, and lab analysis.
# Para-Clinical Sciences
Bridge phase introducing disease mechanisms, drugs, forensic medicine, and initial ward postings.
Pathology
Disease mechanisms, hematopathology, histopathology, and autopsies.
Microbiology
Bacteriology, virology, immunology, and hospital infection control training.
Pharmacology
Drug mechanisms, clinical therapeutics, essential medicines, and prescription writing.
Forensic Medicine & Toxicology
Autopsy procedures, medical jurisprudence, and toxicology diagnostics.
# Clinical Sciences & Specialties
General Medicine & Allied
Internal medicine, cardiology, neurology, psychiatry, and dermatology clinical rotations.
General Surgery & Orthopedics
Surgical procedures, operating theatre experience, trauma care, and orthopedic cast labs.
Obstetrics & Gynecology
Labor ward management, maternal-fetal health, and gynecological surgeries.
Pediatric Care
Neonatal care (NICU), pediatric emergency handling, and childhood growth monitoring.
Community Medicine
Epidemiology, public health centers (PHC) field visits, and preventive health camps.
ENT & Ophthalmology
Ear-Nose-Throat examinations and eye surgical/microsurgical skill labs.
# Compulsory Rotatory Medical Internship (CRMI)
12 Months of continuous hands-on clinical rotation in emergency, medicine, surgery, OB-GYN, pediatrics, and rural health posting before degree award.

# Career Pathways & Post-MBBS Opportunities
Diverse professional avenues awaiting MBBS graduates upon registration
Clinical Medical Practice
Register as a licensed Medical Practitioner (General Physician) in government health centers, private hospitals, or establish private clinics.
International Opportunities
Licensing exams (USMLE, PLAB, etc.) and practice abroad
Postgraduate Specialisation
Pursue M.D. / M.S. clinical specializations, DNB courses, or Super Specialization (M.Ch / D.M.) across clinical and surgical disciplines.
Emergency & Critical Care
Emergency medicine, intensive care, and trauma services
Research & Academia
Medical research, teaching in medical colleges, and academic careers
Public Health & Administration
Government health services, public health programmes, and hospital administration
