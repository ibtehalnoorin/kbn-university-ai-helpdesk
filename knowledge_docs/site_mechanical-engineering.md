Mechanical Engineering is a branch of engineering that involves the design, development, manufacturing, and maintenance of mechanical systems, machines, and devices. It is one of the oldest and broadest engineering disciplines, covering mechanics, thermodynamics, robotics, and materials science.
Courses cover mathematics, physics, and computer-aided design (CAD). Students learn principles of mechanics (statics, dynamics, fluid mechanics), thermodynamics (heat, work, and energy), and materials science — the physical and chemical properties of materials under different conditions.
Specialised areas include robotics and automation, automotive engineering, aerospace engineering, manufacturing engineering, HVAC engineering, and biomechanics. Graduates are prepared for diverse roles across manufacturing, automotive, aerospace, energy, and related industries.
##### Eligibility
PUC / Intermediate from any state or recognised board, or 12th Class from any recognised board, with a minimum of 45% marks in aggregate.
Should have studied Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (or Computer Science / Biology / Electronics / Allied subjects) at this level.
Having cleared the Entrance test held by State Government (Govt. Quota) and KRLMPCA (management quota).
