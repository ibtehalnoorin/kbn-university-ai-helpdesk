# M.D. Microbiology
The Department of Microbiology stands as a beacon of innovation and discovery, dedicated to unraveling the intricacies of microbial life. Our mission is to advance the understanding of microorganisms, exploring their profound impact on health, the environment, and industry. Through both basic and applied research, we delve into diverse areas such as microbial genetics, environmental microbiology, and public health, seeking solutions to pressing global issues. Our faculty, renowned for their expertise, employs state-of-the-art facilities to mentor students, fostering an environment of inquiry and experimentation. We pride ourselves on our interdisciplinary collaborations, working alongside other departments and industries to tackle complex challenges. Committed to education, we offer robust undergraduate and graduate programs that nurture critical thinking and research skills, equipping the next generation of scientists to address global health and environmental dilemmas. At the heart of our endeavors is a commitment to translational research, aiming to transform scientific discoveries into real-world applications that enhance public health and sustainability. Together, we strive to advance scientific knowledge and improve lives, driven by a shared passion for the microbial sciences.
