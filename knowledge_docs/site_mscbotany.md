Botany is also called plant biology or phytology. The study of plants is vital because they support almost all animal life on earth by generating large proportions of oxygen and food. M.Sc. Botany is the study of how plants produce food and how to increase yield.
The programme focuses on the study of weeds, which are a problem in agriculture, and the biology needed to control pathogens. Graduates can work as technical assistants, field assistants and scientific assistants in both private and government sectors. Private firms such as nursery farms and environmental consultancies are also good options for a botanist.
##### Eligibility Criteria
Candidates seeking admission into M.Sc. Botany must meet the following conditions:
Passed B.Sc. degree in Botany or Biological Sciences / Plant Biology as a major/cognate subject from a recognized university.
Secured at least 55% aggregate marks (or equivalent CGPA) in the qualifying undergraduate degree.
Admissions are processed according to University merit guidelines and entrance criteria.

# Specialized Thrust Areas
Core botanical domains driving innovations in agriculture, pharmaceuticals, forestry, and biotechnology
Plant Taxonomy, Systematics & Ethnobotany
Angiosperm classification, molecular phylogenetics, plant nomenclature, herbarium techniques, traditional medicinal plant knowledge, and conservation biodiversity.
Mycology, Plant Pathology & Disease Management
Fungal taxonomy, plant-pathogen interactions, viral and bacterial crop diseases, host resistance mechanisms, and biological pest control.
Plant Tissue Culture & Micropropagation
In-vitro organogenesis, somatic embryogenesis, protoplast isolation, haploid culture, transgenic plant production, and germplasm cryopreservation.
Plant Breeding, Cytogenetics & Biotechnology
Chromosomal aberrations, polyploidy breeding, marker-assisted selection, CRISPR gene editing, and recombinant DNA applications in crops.
Plant Physiology, Biochemistry & Molecular Biology
Photosynthetic carbon assimilation, nitrogen fixation, phytohormone signaling, secondary metabolite pathways, stress physiology, and enzyme kinetics.
Ecology, Phytogeography & Environmental Botany
Ecosystem dynamics, plant community ecology, phytoremediation, climate change impact on flora, and biodiversity hotspot conservation.
# Departmental Infrastructure & Facilities
State-of-the-art tissue culture facilities, herbarium, research laboratories, and botanical gardens
Sterile Plant Tissue Culture Suite
Equipped with laminar air flow hoods, autoclaves, controlled environmental growth chambers, shaker incubators, and sterile transfer rooms.
Institutional Herbarium & Botanical Garden
Rich collection of preserved regional flora specimens, medicinal plant gardens, and greenhouse facilities for experimental plant growth.
Phytochemistry & Microscopic Suite
Outfitted with trinocular microscopes with digital cameras, UV-Vis spectrophotometers, centrifuges, and chromatography columns.
Central Library & E-Journals
Access to international plant science journals, classical botanical monographs, digital databases, and e-learning portals.
# Career Pathways & Industry Scope
Diverse career opportunities across agricultural research, forestry, herbal pharmaceuticals, and academics
Scientific Research Officer (ICAR / CSIR / BSI)
Junior Research Fellow (JRF) and Scientist positions in Botanical Survey of India (BSI), CSIR-CIMAP, NBRI, and ICAR institutes.
Agricultural & Seed Biotechnology Firm
Plant breeders, tissue culture specialists, and crop pathology managers in commercial seed technology and agrochemical companies.
Forestry & Conservation Specialist
Roles in state forest departments, biodiversity boards, national parks, environmental consulting firms, and conservation NGOs.
Academic Professorship & Teaching
Qualify CSIR-NET / GATE / SLET examinations to become Assistant Professors and Lecturers in colleges and universities.
Herbal & Phytopharmaceutical Industry
Phytochemists, quality control analysts, and herbal formulation specialists in Ayurvedic and natural medicine firms.
Doctoral Studies (Ph.D.)
Direct entry into doctoral research in Molecular Plant Sciences, Phytochemistry, or Environmental Biology at KBNU or global institutes.
