The special features of M.Sc. Chemistry include a strong foundation of basics and research components through practical skills, which in turn provide excellent job prospects in academics, industries and other fields of interest.
The programme builds competence to tackle frontier areas such as Supramolecular Chemistry, Sensors, Advanced Materials and Advanced Organic Chemistry.
Graduates are prepared for roles in teaching, research and industry, with the practical and theoretical grounding needed to contribute to modern chemical science and related applications.
##### Eligibility Criteria
Candidates seeking admission into M.Sc. Chemistry must satisfy the following conditions:
Passed B.Sc. degree with Chemistry as one of the major/optional subjects from a recognized university.
Secured at least 55% aggregate marks (or equivalent CGPA) in the qualifying undergraduate degree.
Admissions are processed according to University merit guidelines and regulations.
Note: Please refer to the official admission notification for the academic year for exact cut-offs, reservation details, and application timelines.
# Specialized Thrust Areas
Core chemical science domains driving innovations in healthcare, energy, materials, and environmental sustainability
Organic Synthesis & Medicinal Chemistry
Retrosynthetic analysis, heterocyclic chemistry, drug molecule design, asymmetric synthesis, natural products, and reaction mechanisms.
Physical Chemistry & Chemical Kinetics
Quantum mechanics, chemical thermodynamics, electrochemistry, surface chemistry, catalysis, and statistical thermodynamics.
Coordination & Organometallic Chemistry
Transition metal complexes, bio-inorganic chemistry, organometallic catalysis, crystal field theory, and ligand field dynamics.
Advanced Analytical & Chromatographic Methods
HPLC, GC-MS, ion exchange chromatography, electroanalytical techniques, thermal analysis (TGA/DSC), and validation protocols.
Molecular Spectroscopy & Structure Determination
Structural elucidation using UV-Vis, FTIR, 1H/13C NMR, Mass Spectrometry, X-ray crystallography, and ESR spectroscopy.
Green Chemistry & Polymer Materials
Eco-friendly synthetic routes, solvent-free reactions, nanomaterials synthesis, polymers, and environmental pollution analysis.
# Departmental Infrastructure & Facilities
Equipped with modern instrumental analysis suites, synthetic chemistry setups, and digital tools
Synthesis & Analytical Chemistry Labs
Well-ventilated laboratories fitted with rotary evaporators, fume hoods, digital balances, melting point apparatus, and heating mantles.
Instrumental Spectroscopy Suite
Access to UV-Vis spectrophotometers, FTIR spectrometers, conductivity meters, polarimeters, and analytical chromatography columns.
Chemoinformatics & Computer Lab
Workstation suite equipped with ChemDraw, Gaussian, molecular modeling software, and statistical data processing tools.
Central Library & E-Journals
Access to international ACS, RSC, and Elsevier chemistry research journals, digital databases, and reference volumes.
# Career Pathways & Industry Scope
Expansive professional avenues across pharmaceutical majors, chemical industries, research labs, and academics
Pharmaceutical & Synthetic Research Scientist
Roles in Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) research, drug design, process chemistry, and CROs.
Academic Professorship & Teaching
Qualify CSIR-NET / GATE / SLET exams to become Assistant Professors and Lecturers in colleges and universities.
Quality Control & Assurance (QC/QA) Officer
Analytical chemists managing HPLC/GC testing, method validation, raw material testing, and GMP compliance in factories.
National Defense & Scientific Research (CSIR / DRDO)
Scientific Officer positions in government R&D labs, CSIR institutes, ISRO, DRDO, and BARC.
Materials & Polymer Chemistry Specialist
Develop advanced polymers, catalysts, nanomaterials, paints, dyes, and energy-storage chemical materials.
Doctoral Studies (Ph.D.)
Pursue doctoral research in cutting-edge chemical synthesis, catalysis, or materials science at KBNU or top global universities.
