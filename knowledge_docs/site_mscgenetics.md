Modern Genetics is a research oriented subject, student study how an individual genes or groups of genes are involved in health and disease. The course also provides a detailed understanding of genetic factors and genetic disorders. M.Sc. Genetics focuses on the importance of the history of the field of genetics. The course provides students with knowledge that benefits society, such as the identification and treatment of diseases and genetic disorders and their management.
##### Eligibility
The candidate should have a B.Sc. degree in Genetics, with at least 50% aggregate marks from a recognized institute
