# Ph.D. in Electronics and instrumentation
###### Course Duration : [ Intake: ]
Anchor 1
Department of Electronics and Instrumentation is one of the oldest branches of KBNCE with 34years of track record. The branch was NBA accredited during the period of 2006-2009.It has an intake of 60 with well qualified teaching and non teaching staff, furnished classrooms, fully equipped laboratories, few of which are established by grants from AICTE under MODROB. The faculties at KBNCE provide special attention to individual students both in academics and building career path. From the academic year 2019-20 the department has also started programs in M.Tech in “Applied Electronics and Instrumentation” and Ph.D.
Research Area : Digital Electronics, Power electronics, Wireless sensor Networks, Instrumentation.
