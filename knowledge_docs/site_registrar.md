### REGISTRAR
### Mir Vilayat Ali
### Registrar
### Khaja Bandanawaz University
Mr. Mir Vilayat Ali is a seasoned administrative professional with extensive experience in academic administration. Holding an MBA, he joined Khaja Education Society – A sponsoring body of Khaja Bandanawaz University in 1999 and served as the Superintendent of the Presidential Secretariat.
Under the leadership of H.E. the Chancellor – Syed Muhammad Ali al-Hussaini, and his revered father (the Founder Chancellor), Mr. Mir Vilayat Ali was instrumental in the establishment of Khaja Bandanawaz University, contributing significantly to its formation in 2018. Following the university's inception, he was appointed as Deputy Registrar and was later promoted to Registrar in December 2024.
With over 25 years of experience, Mr. Vilayat Ali had the privilege of working closely with His Holiness, Hazrath Syed Shah Khusro Hussaini (Rh), the Founder Chancellor and then-President of Khaja Education Society. His administrative knowledge and commitment to institutional growth have made him a significant figure in the university's development
Beyond his professional achievements, he is known for his leadership qualities, meticulous attention to detail, and ability to navigate complex administrative challenges with ease. He is known for his integrity, dedication, and ability to foster a collaborative work environment. A problem-solver by nature, he approach every task with a strategic mindset and a deep sense of responsibility. His warm and approachable personality has earned him the trust and admiration of colleagues, students, and faculty alike.
